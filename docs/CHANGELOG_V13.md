# V13 — liquid glass and uninterrupted content

15 October 2026. This pass follows the request for matching Home glass actions, clearer menu/search animation, smaller phone layouts and automatic content without media controls.

- Both Home Programmes and Admissions actions use the same clear glass, lit edge, native blur, press response and reflected hover sweep. Header, dock, footer and guide share translucent materials instead of separate dark fills.
- Search grows from its actual trigger on phone and desktop, with staged text resolution and a brief reflected sweep. Quick access springs from the phone emblem; the page blurs behind a separate layer so its colours remain visible. College navigation uses the same overlay glass.
- News, campus photographs, recognitions, recruiters and community voices advance every five seconds of active viewing. Next/previous/pause controls and clickable pagination are removed. Native scrolling, swipe and keyboard arrows remain; expanded quotes, focus/hover, overlays, hidden/offscreen pages and motion preferences hold rotation.
- Manual movement restarts the viewing period. Pointer cancellation/window blur release a held swipe, and replacing a focused quote clears stale focus state. Reading a full quote holds it until collapsed and focus/hover leave.
- Removed photograph fullscreen actions across the shared photo component and journal images, and unmounted the unused lightbox. Documentary source links remain available.
- The vision icon and label share a row. Phone discipline rows combine icon/title, use smaller text and less empty height; news banners are shorter and the header/dock have compact safe-area spacing.
- Added a shimmer during actual photo loading and a softer decoded-image reveal. There is no artificial introductory loading delay. Reduced-motion behavior remains.

Institutional text, placement figures, source portraits, event dates and the vibrant authentic campus film are unchanged. See [design contract](../DESIGN.md) and [verification](VERIFICATION.md).

Verification: 12 responsive/theme cases, six focused motion cases, three material/loading checks, two emulated touch devices, three navigation cases, five film cases, five static entries and source-integrity checks passed.
